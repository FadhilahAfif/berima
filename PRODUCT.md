# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Berima is used by students who need small digital services and students who provide those services for income or portfolio growth. They use the app in quick, task-oriented sessions: browsing layanan, comparing trust signals, placing orders, managing work, chatting, submitting results, reviewing transactions, and maintaining profile credibility through verification and portfolio evidence.

## Product Purpose

Berima helps student-to-student micro-service transactions move out of informal chat groups into a structured, trustworthy Android marketplace. Success means users can find relevant layanan quickly, understand pricing and status clearly, trust penyedia jasa through reviews, badges, and portfolios, and complete an order flow without confusion or unnecessary friction.

## Brand Personality

Approachable, grounded, and trustworthy. The interface should feel calm enough for late-night student use, professional enough to support paid services, and simple enough that first-time users can complete core flows without instruction.

## Anti-references

Avoid over-decorated mobile UI, oversized icons or display text in task surfaces, duplicated primary buttons with the same visual weight, destructive actions styled like positive save actions, nested card-heavy layouts, generic marketplace retail wording, and any pattern that makes a student service workflow feel like a template rather than a reliable tool.

## Design Principles

1. Trust should be visible at decision points through badges, reviews, profile completeness, service policy, and clear transaction status.
2. Marketplace actions should be calm and unambiguous, with primary, secondary, and destructive actions visually separated by role and risk.
3. The product should stay service-first in language, using layanan, pemesan, penyedia jasa, and portofolio in user-facing copy.
4. Each screen should lead with the user's next task, not decoration, marketing copy, or repeated card framing.
5. Price, status, and ownership cues should be scannable before details, because they drive confidence in a mobile marketplace.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA-equivalent readability for mobile UI: strong text contrast, minimum 44dp touch targets, readable layouts under Android font scaling, clear focus and enabled states, and motion that supports state changes without delaying core tasks.
